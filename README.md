🌍 CarbonIQ — AI-Powered Industrial Sustainability Intelligence Platform

CarbonIQ is an advanced AI-powered sustainability platform that helps industries and organizations monitor, analyze, and reduce their carbon emissions using real-time analytics and conversational AI.

The platform combines:

🤖 Artificial Intelligence
🏭 Industrial Sustainability
📊 Carbon Footprint Monitoring
🌱 ESG Intelligence
💬 Conversational AI Chatbot

into one modern enterprise-level solution.

✅ AI-Powered Sustainability Analysis
Analyze industrial emissions using AI
Generate smart sustainability recommendations
Environmental risk detection
ESG improvement suggestions
✅ Conversational AI Chatbot

Users can interact naturally with CarbonIQ AI.

AI automatically:

analyzes emissions
estimates environmental impact
suggests sustainability improvements
